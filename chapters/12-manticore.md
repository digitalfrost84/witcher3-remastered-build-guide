# 12. Manticore: the School of the Manticore

> Decoctions, bombs and crits: the alchemist's school, and the one that takes longest to come together.

## Lore

The School of the Manticore lived at the far eastern edge of the witcher world, on the border of the Korath desert, in two castles built with Zerrikanian money: **Behelt Nar** in the west and **Bialsuf Alsarea** in the east. Its other name was **Alqatil**, "the Slayers". Northerners knew almost nothing about it ([Witcher wiki: School of the Manticore](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/School_of_the_Manticore)).

It was founded by **Iwan**, later called Imad Asem, who left the Bear school during its schism. His band escorted caravans, saved a royal Zerrikanian caravan from manticores and brought the court mage **Nasira Faizan** home safely, which won them royal patronage. Iwan and Nasira tried to recreate the witcher mutations, and the school became known for defense and potions, and for shields of meteorite steel and silver. Most of its witchers died in 1146 on the "Day of Fire", when a fire elemental struck a royal caravan; Zerrikania shunned the survivors, and by Geralt's time the few left hunt monsters and winter at Behelt Nar.

**In Blood and Wine,** the Manticore diagrams belonged to **Merten**, a Manticore who converted to the faith of Lebioda and became a priest named Shavel. Geralt had seen Manticore armor only once before, incomplete and worn ([Witcher wiki: Scavenger Hunt: Grandmaster Manticore Gear](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Scavenger_Hunt:_Grandmaster_Manticore_Gear)).

## How it plays

A Manticore treats **Toxicity as a resource, not a limit**. Decoctions lock 50 Toxicity each for 30 minutes (chapter 4), and this build stacks as many as it can afford: Acquired Tolerance and Metabolic Control raise the ceiling, Adaptability makes each decoction last longer, Tissue Transmutation adds Vitality while they run, and Endure Pain adds more once Toxicity passes the safe threshold. In Blood and Wine, **Euphoria** turns all that Toxicity into sword damage and Sign intensity, and the Manticore set gives every potion, bomb and decoction an extra charge and lets bombs crit.

Before then, Manticore plays as a **potion-and-bomb swordsman** in medium armor: Manticore School Techniques boosts sword and bomb damage, a bomb opens the fight, Element of Surprise powers up the melee that follows, and potions heal through Refreshment.

### Late game: level 30+ and Blood and Wine

- **Delayed Recovery** (Alchemy, from Tissue Transmutation) to rank 3. Sources describe it two ways: potion effects that don't expire while your potion Toxicity stays above 85/70/55% of the maximum (witcherhour), or each potion you drink above 70% Toxicity extending the others by 5 seconds (WitcherDB). Either way it rewards exactly what this build does.
- **Rend** (Combat, from Razor Focus) to rank 2: the finisher for your Adrenaline.
- **Rank 3:** Metabolic Control (+30), Adaptability (decoctions last twice as long: an hour each), Endure Pain (+30%), Tissue Transmutation (+900), Refreshment (30% per dose), Synergy.
- **Mutation:** Toxic Blood, then Euphoria.

**What to skip.** **Fast Metabolism** drains Toxicity faster, which works against Euphoria, and **Side Effects** can only be reached through it. KeenGamer skips both; Hack the Minotaur's expert Euphoria build takes Fast Metabolism ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/the-witcher-3-remastered-best-builds-for-every-playstyle/); [Hack the Minotaur](https://hacktheminotaur.com/the-witcher-3/the-witcher-3-remastered-euphoria-build/)). **High Tolerance** adds crit damage from your Toxicity but makes you take 150% damage at 80% Toxicity or more, where a Euphoria build spends its time. It's for experts only.

## Slots, mutagens and mutation

| Group | Skills | Mutagen |

| Mutation slots (green) | Frenzy, Hunter Instinct, Pyrotechnics, Efficiency | — |

**Delusion:** if you want Axii's dialogue options, it takes Endure Pain's slot.

**Mutation: Euphoria** (Toxic Blood, then Euphoria: 5 Ability Points and 5 greater green mutagens). +0.75% sword damage and Sign intensity for every point of Toxicity. Two research steps open the first extra slot, and it takes Alchemy skills. Sources disagree on its cap (chapter 5), and FinalBoss and VGTimes say 5.0 weakened it, though no patch note says so ([FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-how-to-make-an-alchemy-build-remastered-5-0)).

**The Levity alternative.** With a tier 3 Runewright, the **Levity** glyphword on your Manticore chest makes all your armor count as light (chapter 6). That lets you run **Cat School Techniques** instead of Manticore's: +96% crit damage and +24% fast attack damage at rank 3, against Manticore's +24% sword and bomb damage. KeenGamer's and Console Pulse's 5.0 Manticore builds both do this ([Console Pulse: Spin to Whirl build](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-remastered-spin-to-whirl-build)). It's the stronger choice if your swords crit often; keep Manticore's own Technique if you throw a lot of bombs.

## Consumables and the fight loop

**Decoctions.** FinalBoss suggests **Ekimmara, Katakan and Archgriffin** for offense, or **Ekimmara and Nightwraith** for defense. Ekimmara heals you from the damage you deal, Katakan adds 10% crit chance, Archgriffin turns strong attacks into a 5%-of-Vitality chunk against big targets, and Nightwraith adds max Vitality with every kill until you meditate. **Potions:** Thunderbolt, Swallow, **Full Moon** (the superior version also heals Vitality equal to your Toxicity, which for this build is a lot), White Raffard's. **Bombs:** Grapeshot and Dancing Star for damage, Samum and Northern Wind for control.

**The loop:**

1. **Before the fight:** decoctions running (an hour each with Adaptability at rank 3), oil on the blade, Thunderbolt.
2. **Open with a bomb.** It sets up Element of Surprise, and with the Grandmaster set it can crit and leaves your hand without delay.
3. **Quen,** then fight with fast attacks into strong attacks.
4. **Drink freely.** Every dose heals through Refreshment and adds Toxicity, which Euphoria turns into damage. Just stay under your maximum.
5. **Finish with Rend** once Razor Focus and your hits have filled the bar.

## Gear path

| Levels | Armor | Swords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–10 | Any medium armor: **Armor of a Thousand Flowers** (7) or **White Tiger of the West** (11) if your account has them; **Nilfgaardian** (10) | Whatever has the most damage |
| 11–39 | **Griffin**, **Wolven** or **Forgotten Wolven**, whichever tier is newest (chapters 9 and 11). All are medium, so they feed Manticore School Techniques | The matching witcher swords, or **Viper Venomous** at 39 |
| 40+ | **Grandmaster Manticore**, all six pieces (Toussaint; crafted by Lazare Lafargue) | Both Manticore swords: +250 armor piercing, +50% crit damage, +5% crit chance |

**Getting the set.** Manticore exists only at Grandmaster level, so its recipes don't need a lower-tier piece. The diagrams come from *Scavenger Hunt: Grandmaster Manticore Gear*, which opens through Lafargue's quest *Master Master Master Master!* in Beauclair ([WitcherHour: Manticore armor](https://witcherhour.com/get-manticore-armor-bw/); [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/witcher-3-how-to-get-manticore-gear-set); [Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-manticore-gear-guide)). The set is **medium** armor: one Remastered guide calls it light, almost certainly because its screenshot showed a chest enchanted with Levity (chapter 3).

**Why all six.** Each armor piece adds +5 max Toxicity (+20 for four). The 3-piece bonus lets bombs crit and removes their throwing delay; the 6-piece bonus gives **every alchemy item +1 maximum charge**, so one more Swallow, one more bomb and one more dose of each decoction per meditation ([Witcher wiki: Manticore School Gear](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Manticore_School_Gear)). For an alchemist that's the defining bonus, and the Manticore swords are strong in their own right, so keep the full set.

## The maths

![How many decoctions fit at different maximum Toxicity levels: one with no skills, two with Acquired Tolerance rank 1, up to four with Acquired Tolerance rank 3](../images/toxicity-budget.png)

**The Toxicity budget.** A decoction locks 50 Toxicity until it ends, so the number you can run is your maximum, minus room for a potion or two, divided by 50. Acquired Tolerance is the big lever because it scales with every recipe you know: at 40 recipes, rank 3 adds 120. With Metabolic Control rank 3 (+30) and four Manticore pieces (+20) on top, a maximum around 270 fits four decoctions with 70 to spare. The base of 100 is the next-gen value; 5.0's isn't confirmed.

**What Euphoria does with it.** At +0.75% per point:

| Toxicity held | Euphoria bonus to sword damage and Sign intensity |
| --- | --- |
| 50 (one decoction) | +37.5% |
| 100 (two) | +75% |
| 150 (three) | +112.5% |
| 200 (four) | +150% |

```math
\text{Euphoria bonus} = 0.75\% \times \text{current Toxicity}
```

If the cap is a flat 75%, two decoctions already max it out and the rest is sustain. If it rises with your maximum Toxicity, as KeenGamer says, every extra point of Toxicity room is also damage. Either way, treat these as upper bounds until the 5.0 tooltip confirms them: two guides report that 5.0 weakened Euphoria. For comparison, Thunderbolt adds +30% attack power.

## Verdict

**Strengths.** The highest ceiling of any school once everything is in place. Hack the Minotaur calls its Euphoria build the highest damage ceiling in the game; FinalBoss calls the alchemy build the strongest for Death March once its kit is assembled; VGTimes says three decoctions make Geralt very hard to kill even on Death March; KeenGamer calls it the most durable build once recipes are in place.

**Weaknesses.** Everything arrives late: the set at level 40, Euphoria in Blood and Wine, Acquired Tolerance's full value only after you've learned dozens of recipes. Hack the Minotaur rates its Euphoria build 5 out of 5 for difficulty and weak early. It's the most preparation-heavy school, and a Toxicity mistake mid-fight is expensive.

**Who it suits:** players who enjoy the alchemy system for its own sake, and anyone planning a long Blood and Wine endgame or New Game+.
