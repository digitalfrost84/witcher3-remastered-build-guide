# 10. Ursine: the School of the Bear

> Heavy armor, strong attacks, and a Quen shield that never quite breaks.

## Lore

The School of the Bear was founded in defiance. **Arnaghad** nearly killed a fellow witcher and chose to fight the Order rather than accept its punishment. The school he built saw codes and knightly virtue as vanity and valued autonomy above everything. Bears were loners, and other schools called them "kinslaying Bears" ([Witcher wiki: School of the Bear](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/School_of_the_Bear)).

Their keep, **Haern Caduch**, sat high in the snowy Amell Mountains, and its life was hard. In the "trial of the mountain", students climbed Mount Gorgon to bring back a runestone, and many froze to death on the way. The survivors had unusual endurance. They fought in heavy armor (a quilted gambeson, mail to the knees, plate on the shoulders) and their method was to absorb a blow, then close in or step back and strike. Bears and Cats are the two schools known to have used crossbows.

The school ended the way it lived: after a failed contract against a vampire cabal, local villagers drove the Bears out, and with little loyalty to each other they scattered rather than defend the keep. Two later schools grew out of a split among the Bears: the **Manticore** and the **Viper** (chapters 12 and 13). Much of this history comes from Gwent and the tabletop RPG rather than the novels.

**In The Witcher 3,** the Ursine gear belonged to **Gerd**, a witcher-adventurer, and a ballad in the dungeon beneath the castle on An Skellig tells what became of him ([Witcher wiki: Scavenger Hunt: Bear School Gear](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Scavenger_Hunt:_Bear_School_Gear)).

## How it plays

An Ursine build trades speed for staying power. **Heavy armor** feeds Bear School Techniques with extra Vitality and strong attack damage. **Fast attacks charge strong attacks** through Strength Training, Crushing Blow can make the next two strong attacks hit 50% harder, and Sunder Armor strips the target's resistance with every strong hit. **Quen** is the second layer of armor: Active Shield heals you from what it absorbs, and the Grandmaster set recasts it for free.

Adrenaline has two uses, and the build changes when you get Blood and Wine:

- **Before mutations, spend it.** Rend turns a full bar into one strike that ignores defenses, +30% damage per point at rank 3. FinalBoss and VGTimes play Ursine this way.
- **With Mutated Skin, hold it.** Each point you hold cuts the damage you take by 15%, up to 45% at a full bar. KeenGamer's Death March tank never spends Adrenaline at all.

The cost is Stamina: heavy armor regenerates it more slowly, so you cast fewer Signs, and Active Shield's upkeep competes with everything else ([FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-how-to-build-an-ursine-tank-remastered-5-0)).

### Late game: level 30+ and Blood and Wine

- **Tissue Transmutation** (Alchemy, from Acquired Tolerance) to rank 3: +300/600/900 max Vitality while any decoction is active.
- **Survival Instinct** (General, from Anger Management) to rank 3: +8/16/24% max Vitality.
- **Rank 3:** Sunder Armor (three stacks), Resolve (no Adrenaline lost when hit), Active Shield (no Stamina drain), Synergy.
- **Rank 2:** Undying, Razor Focus, Rend.
- **Deadly Precision** (Combat, from Rend): any attack can set up an instant-kill strong attack; enemies immune to it give you Adrenaline instead.
- **Mutation:** Toxic Blood → Euphoria → Mutated Skin (below).

## Slots, mutagens and mutation

| Group | Skills | Mutagen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strength Training, Crushing Blow, Sunder Armor | Red |
| 2 | Resolve, Razor Focus, Undying | Red |
| 3 | Bear School Techniques, Synergy, Survival Instinct | Red (base bonus only) |
| 4 | Active Shield, Refreshment, Tissue Transmutation | Green |
| Mutation slots (red or green) | Rend or Deadly Precision, then Muscle Memory | — |

**Delusion:** if you want Axii's dialogue options, it takes Refreshment's slot.

**Mutation: Mutated Skin** (Toxic Blood, Euphoria, then Mutated Skin: 10 Ability Points, 8 greater green and 2 greater red mutagens). −15% damage taken per Adrenaline point held, up to −45%; spending points lowers it. KeenGamer says it needs Euphoria researched first; Gamer Guides lists no prerequisite ([KeenGamer: Mutations](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/the-witcher-3-remastered-all-mutations-how-to-unlock-them/)). It's a two-color mutation, so its extra slots take Combat or Alchemy skills.

Later, **Second Life** gives a full heal at 0 Vitality on a long cooldown, but its research path runs through Bloodbath, Piercing Cold and Adrenaline Rush: 22 points in all (chapter 5). For most players Undying does the same job for one point.

## Consumables and the fight loop

**Decoctions.** **Archgriffin**: strong attacks spend all your Stamina, then remove 5% of the target's Vitality, which is enormous against bosses with huge health bars; cast Quen before you start swinging. **Griffin decoction**: +1% resistance per hit taken, up to 25% per fight. **Troll** and **Ekimmara** for healing. Any decoction also switches on Tissue Transmutation. **Potions:** Swallow, Full Moon (its superior version also heals Vitality equal to your Toxicity), and White Raffard's for emergencies.

**The loop:**

1. **Quen first,** Active Shield against groups. Let the shield take hits that would otherwise drain your Adrenaline.
2. **Fast attacks, then a strong attack.** The fast hits charge Strength Training; the strong hit can trigger Crushing Blow and stacks Sunder Armor.
3. **Keep Sunder Armor stacked** on the target you're fighting; three stacks strip 30% of its damage resistance.
4. **Spend or hold.** Before Mutated Skin, finish isolated or big enemies with a full-bar Rend. After it, hold three points and let the damage reduction carry you.
5. **Undying** is the last line, with Razor Focus making sure there's always at least one point in the bar.

Location guides: [Mobalytics: Ursine School gear](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/witcher-3-how-to-get-ursine-school-gear-set), [Console Pulse: Ursine gear](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-ursine-gear). Ursine is the most defensive witcher set; its Grandmaster chest also adds +22% Adrenaline gain ([Fextralife: Grandmaster Ursine Armor](https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Grandmaster+Ursine+Armor)).

**The set bonus and Quen.** The 3-piece bonus gives a broken Quen a 5% chance per set piece to recast for free (30% with all six); the 6-piece bonus makes Quen-based abilities deal +200% damage. Since patch 5.01, Exploding Shield also reflects damage, so the 6-piece bonus should now have more to multiply; that's this book's reading of the two changes together, not something a source has tested. Hack the Minotaur describes a different, strong-attack-and-Adrenaline Ursine bonus after a 5.0 "gear rework" that no patch note mentions, so read the tooltip before you craft (chapter 3).

**Other heavy armor in Blood and Wine** (chapter 16): **Tesham Mutna** (level 39, missable during *La Cage au Fou*; kills heal you with 3+ pieces), **Hen Gaidth** or the **Toussaint relic set** (one or the other, depending on your story route), and the crafted **Toussaint Knight's Tourney** armor (level 48, +500 Vitality, Quen deflects projectiles 25% of the time). The **Heft** glyphword (chapter 6) makes any armor count as heavy, if your favorite set isn't.

## The maths

**Effective health.** How much punishment you can take scales with your Vitality divided by the share of each hit that reaches it. With Bear School Techniques rank 3 on four heavy pieces (+24%) and Survival Instinct rank 3 (+24%), and assuming the two add up, your Vitality is 1.48 times what it would be without them. Mutated Skin then divides the damage:

| Adrenaline held | Damage taken (Mutated Skin) | Hits to go down, compared with no bonuses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 100% | 1.48× |
| 1 | 85% | 1.74× |
| 2 | 70% | 2.11× |
| 3 | 55% | 2.69× |

```math
\text{effective health} = \frac{1 + 0.24 + 0.24}{1 - 0.15 \times \text{Adrenaline held}}
```

Quen, armor, resistances, the Griffin decoction and Tissue Transmutation all come on top. The table also shows why Rend and Mutated Skin pull against each other: a rank-3 Rend at a full bar adds +90% to one strike, while holding the same three points nearly doubles how long you last (2.69× against 1.48×).

## Verdict

**Strengths.** The most forgiving school. KeenGamer and FinalBoss both call it the safest first Death March build ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/the-witcher-3-remastered-best-builds-for-every-playstyle/); [FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-choose-the-best-remastered-build-by-playstyle)). The loop is simple, and mistakes cost you Quen and some Vitality instead of the fight.

**Weaknesses.** It starts slowly, because Bear School Techniques needs heavy armor and the Ursine set arrives at level 20. Heavy armor slows Stamina regeneration. And the damage ceiling is the lowest of the six: Rend is the main boss tool, and once Mutated Skin asks you to stop using it, fights get long ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/guides/169608-best-builds-in-the-witcher-3-remastered-skills-and-progression.html)).

**Who it suits:** a first Death March run, or anyone who'd rather trade blows than dance.
