# 18. Missables: one-time gear

Most gear in The Witcher 3 waits for you, including every witcher school diagram. A few things don't. This chapter lists the gear you can lose for good, with exactly what loses it. Patch 5.0 changed no items, so pre-5.0 sources apply ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/witcher-3-remastered-patch-notes-skill-reset-reforge-and-major-changes/)). One thing 5.0 adds: since owning an item or its diagram unlocks its look for Reforge, every missable below is also a look you can never reforge to.

**Make a manual save** before each point of no return marked below.

## Base game

- [ ] **Winter's Blade** (relic steel). Ask Crach an Craite for help during *Brothers in Arms: Skellige*; he can't send men and gives you the sword instead. You lose it if you sail to the Isle of Mists first. The Witcher wiki adds that he only gives it if you helped his children, Cerys in *Possession* and Hjalmar in *The Lord of Undvik*; GameBanshee and Gamer Guides mention no condition. Finish both quests first to be safe ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Brothers_In_Arms:_Skellige); [GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/brothersinarms.php)).
- [ ] **Hjalmar's steel sword.** During *King's Gambit*, win the fistfight in Hjalmar's corner of the Kaer Trolde feast hall before you leave to find Crach ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/kingsgambit.php)).
- [ ] **Rose of Shaerrawed** (silver). While preparing for the Battle of Kaer Morhen, choose the armory in the workshop instead of patching the wall. GameBanshee names the sword; Gamer Guides says the armory gives a random silver sword ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/thebattleofkaermorhen.php)).

**Not missable:** the Feline, Griffin, Ursine, Wolven and Forgotten Wolven diagrams. None is tied to a quest that can fail, the open world stays playable after the ending, and the Isle of Mists has no unique loot. Kaer Morhen holds several of them; no source says outright that it stays open after the battle there, but every guide treats it as reachable ([Console Pulse: Wolf gear](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-wolf-school-gear-guide); [Gamer Guides: Epilogue](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/something-ends-something-begins)).

## Hearts of Stone

- [ ] **Viper armor diagrams** (all four). **Countess Mignole** sells them at the auction house at the start of *Open Sesame!*, while Vimme Vivaldi introduces you to the guests. Buy them before the auction ends; you can't return. One old source links them to Yaromir's optional art test; others don't, but the test is free, so do it anyway ([GameBanshee: Open Sesame!](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/opensesame.php); [Console Pulse: HoS missables](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-achievements-missables-guide)).
- [ ] **Auction lots.** Professor's Spectacles (cosmetic), *Starry Night over the Pontar* (with Yaromir's tip, it starts *Avid Collector*) and Count Romilly's Figurine (dismantling it starts *A Dark Legacy*). Bring at least 1,000 crowns.
- [ ] **Viper Venomous steel sword diagram.** In a container in the inner vault during the Borsodi heist. Loot every container before you leave the vault; you can't go back.
- [ ] **Caretaker's Spade.** Dropped by the Caretaker in the garden during *Scenes From a Marriage*. It doesn't glow in Witcher Senses; pick it up before you go into the house ([Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-remastered-caretaker-spade-iris-aerondight)).
- [ ] **Ornate robe and boots** (relic). In the container next to Iris's body in the master bedroom, same quest.
- [ ] **Viper Venomous silver sword and Iris.** Both depend on the ending of *Whatsoever a Man Soweth…*:
  1. Do the optional step with **Shani and Professor Shakeslock** first, or you won't be offered the challenge.
  2. At the Temple of Lilvani, choose to **help Olgierd** in the timed choice.
  3. In O'Dimm's riddle world, kill the specter at the red-glowing spot and **pull the sword from the stone before you solve the riddle**. GameBanshee gives the timer as about five minutes.
  4. Win, and Olgierd gives you **Iris** back in the real world.

  If you let O'Dimm take Olgierd instead, you get one boon (the Caparison of Lament, the Horn of Plenty, the Bottomless Carafe or 5,000 crowns) and lose both swords ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Whatsoever_a_Man_Soweth...); [GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/whatsoeveramansoweth.php)).

**Not missable:** the Ofieri diagrams (an old bug that could stall the quest was fixed in patch 4.0), the Runewright, and the New Moon relic set in the open world. Only the weaker New Moon copies you wear during the heist are one-time.

## Blood and Wine

- [ ] **Tesham Mutna set** (six pieces). Only during *La Cage au Fou*, in three containers in the ruins on the way to the cage chamber: the chest Regis leans on (armor, trousers, sword), the chest by the door after the stairs beside the hanging cages (mask, boots), and a strongbox on a wall shelf after the second cell (gauntlets) ([GoSuNoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/witcher-3/tesham-mutna-armor/); [Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Tesham_Mutna_armor)).
- [ ] **Tourney prizes** in *The Warble of a Smitten Knight*: a crossbow, a saddle and a leveled sword, plus easy Honor and Valor chances for Aerondight. The quest fails once *Capture the Castle* starts (Console Pulse) or when you find Guillaume's body during *The Night of Long Fangs* (GameBanshee); either way, finish it before the story's route split ([Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-warble-of-a-smitten-knight-guide)).
- [ ] **The route choice in *The Night of Long Fangs*** decides which heavy relic set you can get:

  | Route | You get | It locks when |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | **Find Damien → Syanna** (*Beyond Hill and Dale…*) | The **Toussaint relic** armor, **Vitis** and the found **Gesheft**, all in the Land of a Thousand Fables | You interact with the Land of a Thousand Fables book in the palace playroom wardrobe |
  | **Find Orianna → the Unseen Elder** (*Blood Simple*, then *What Lies Unseen*) | The **Hen Gaidth** set and the **Cantata** silver sword | You tell Regis at the docks that you're going to the Unseen Elder (per Console Pulse) |

  You can follow both leads before you commit. One set per playthrough: a manual save before the choice is the only way to see both ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Night_of_Long_Fangs); [Console Pulse: B&W choices](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-choices-blood-and-wine)).
- [ ] **Loot as you go** in the Land of a Thousand Fables and the Unseen Elder's lair. Both close behind you.

**Not missable in practice:** **Aerondight**. Every virtue has several chances, and the quest can be finished after the main story (chapter 17). **Not missable:** every Grandmaster diagram; even Manticore's trial in Murky Delve can't be failed ([Console Pulse: Grandmaster sets](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-grandmaster-gear-sets-bonuses-compared)).

## Points of no return in the main story

- **The Isle of Mists.** Near the arch, the game asks you to make a manual save, and GameBanshee warns that most open base-game side quests fail when you continue. This is also the deadline for Winter's Blade. Finish the *Brothers in Arms* quests in Velen, Novigrad and Skellige before you sail ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/theisleofmists.php); [Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/the-wild-hunt/the-isle-of-mists)).
- **The final one:** speaking to Avallac'h next to the boat during *On Thin Ice* ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/On_Thin_Ice)).
- **After the ending** you return to the open world and can keep playing contracts, treasure hunts and side quests, and both expansions ([Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/something-ends-something-begins)).
