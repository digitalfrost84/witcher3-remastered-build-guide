# 6. Enchanting (Hearts of Stone)

Every weapon and armor piece can carry upgrades in its slots. Single **runestones** and **glyphs** are cheap and available from the start. Hearts of Stone adds the **Runewright**, who combines three of them into a **runeword** or **glyphword**, an effect strong enough to change how a build plays. Three glyphwords matter more than the rest because they change your armor's weight class, and with it which School Technique your armor feeds.

No 5.0 or 5.01 patch note mentions enchanting. The values below come from pre-5.0 sources; the 4.0 next-gen update changed several of them, and where it did, the newer value is shown ([Console Pulse: Runewords and glyphwords ranked](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-runewords-glyphwords-ranked)).

## Runestones and glyphs

**Runestones go in weapons and glyphs go in armor.** Each comes in lesser, normal and greater grades, and an item has up to three slots ([Fextralife: Upgrades](https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Upgrades)). The ones builds use most:

| Upgrade | Goes in | Effect (lesser / normal / greater) | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chernobog** runestone | Swords | +2 / 3 / 5% attack power | Sword builds |
| **Veles** runestone | Swords | +2 / 3 / 5% Sign intensity | Sign builds |
| **Sign glyphs** (Aard, Igni, Yrden, Quen, Axii) | Armor | +2 / 5 / 10% intensity of that Sign | Sign builds |
| **Mending** glyph | Armor | +1 / 2 / 3 Vitality regeneration | Anyone |

A blacksmith sockets runestones and an armorer sockets glyphs, for a small fee. **Removing upgrades** keeps the item but destroys the stones; **dismantling** the item destroys it but gives the stones back ([Fextralife: Upgrades](https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Upgrades)). Single stones also survive when you craft the next tier of a witcher item, so you can socket them early without losing anything ([Witcher wiki: Glyph](https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Glyph)).

## The Runewright

The Runewright is an Ofieri mage-craftsman who sets up shop at **Upper Mill**, in the far Novigrad outskirts near Brunwich ([Fextralife: Runewords](https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Runewords)).

1. **Enchanting: Start-up Costs.** Take the notice "A Runewright Upon Your Shores Has Landed" from the Seven Cats Inn, or talk to him directly, and pay **5,000 crowns**. Suggested level 32 ([guides4gamers](https://guides4gamers.com/witcher-3-wild-hunt/quest/enchanting-start-up-costs/)).
2. **Enchanting: Quality Has Its Price.** Take the pickaxe from the barn next to his tent and mine the large piece of jade in Bloodrot Pit, which arachnomorphs guard ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/enchanting-qualityhasitsprice.php)).
3. **Two more investments.** **10,000 crowns** for tier 2 and **15,000 crowns** for tier 3: 30,000 in total.

| Tier | Total paid | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5,000 | Words from lesser stones: 3 runewords, 5 glyphwords |
| 2 | 15,000 | Words from normal stones: 3 runewords, 5 glyphwords |
| 3 | 30,000 | Words from greater stones: 3 runewords, 4 glyphwords |

He also adds slots to items, and each extra slot costs more than the last; one player paid about 2,800 crowns for three slots on a sword ([PC Games](https://www.pcgames.de/The-Witcher-3-Hearts-of-Stone-Spiel-56201/Tipps/Runenschmied-Guide-1174577/)). Aerondight starts with no slots. Console Pulse thinks most players can stop at tier 2. This book disagrees for any build that wants **Levity**, which is tier 3.

## Rules for words

- **Runewords go only on steel and silver swords; glyphwords only on chest armor.**
- **The item needs three slots, and the word fills all three.** One word per item, and no single stones next to it. Sources disagree on whether the slots must be empty first or the stones in them are destroyed; either way you lose them.
- **A runeword only works on the sword you have drawn.** Preservation is the exception.
- **Crafting the next tier of witcher gear destroys words** (single stones survive). Put words on your final pieces.

Sources: [Fextralife: Glyphwords](https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Glyphwords), [Console Pulse: Runewright costs](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-runewright-costs-best-enchantments-guide), [Gamestegy: Replenishment](https://gamestegy.com/witcher-3/wiki/1397/replenishment).

## The three weight glyphwords

These three turn chest-armor enchanting into a build tool. Each makes **all the armor you wear** count as one weight class, so every piece feeds the School Technique of that weight:

| Glyphword | Tier | All armor counts as | Feeds | Recipe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Heft** | 1 | Heavy | Bear | Lesser Quen, Mending, Reinforcement |
| **Balance** | 2 | Medium | Wolf, Griffin, Manticore, Viper | Axii, Mending, Reinforcement |
| **Levity** | 3 | Light (and keeps its resistances) | Cat | Greater Aard, Mending, Reinforcement |

What this buys you is the freedom to pick armor for its stats and set bonus, then pick your Technique separately. Two combinations stand out:

- **Levity on medium or heavy armor** gives you Cat School Techniques (+24% crit damage per piece at rank 3, so +96% with four pieces) while you keep the heavier armor's protection. Two 5.0 Manticore builds, from Console Pulse and KeenGamer, run Cat School Techniques this way (KeenGamer's describes the Levity effect without naming the glyphword) ([Console Pulse: Spin to Whirl build](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-remastered-spin-to-whirl-build); [Console Pulse: Best enchantment builds](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-best-enchantment-builds-four-setups)).
- **Balance on light or heavy armor** lets a Sign build wear whatever has the best Sign stats and still use Griffin School Techniques.

## All runewords

| Runeword | Tier | Recipe | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Preservation** | 1 | Lesser Svarog, Devana, Morana | Armorer's table and grindstone bonuses never expire |
| **Dumplings** | 1 | 2 Pyerog, 1 Tvarog | Food heals 400% more (100% before 4.0) |
| **Placation** | 1 | Lesser Stribog, Devana, Morana | Reaching full Adrenaline drains it to 0; while it drains, Vitality and Stamina regenerate faster and Toxicity drops faster |
| **Rejuvenation** | 2 | Perun, Svarog, Stribog | Each fatal blow restores 100% Stamina (25% before 4.0) |
| **Elation** | 2 | Dazhbog, Veles, Devana | Fatal sword blows give 1 Adrenaline point |
| **Severance** | 2 | Zoria, Veles, Perun | Whirl reaches 1.1 yards further, Rend 1.9 yards |
| **Invigoration** | 3 | Greater Devana, Zoria, Perun | At full Vitality, regeneration becomes bonus damage on your next hit, up to +50% |
| **Prolongation** | 3 | Greater Morana, Perun, Svarog | Each unblocked hit you take extends active potions by 1 second |
| **Replenishment** | 3 | Greater Triglav, Morana, Dazhbog | Casting a Sign spends 1 Adrenaline; your next sword hit carries that Sign's effect |

## Picks for each school

| School | Chest (glyphword) | Swords (runeword or stones) | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feline** | Levity, if your best armor isn't light | Severance if you use Whirl; otherwise three greater Chernobog, or Invigoration for burst | Placation, which throws away the Adrenaline Battle Frenzy needs |
| **Griffin** | Entanglement for a Yrden build; Rotation or Eruption for Igni; Balance if your armor isn't medium | Three greater Veles | — |
| **Ursine** | Protection or Retribution; Heft if your armor isn't heavy | Severance for Rend, or greater Chernobog | Levity, unless you switch to Cat |
| **Wolven** | Protection | A mix of Chernobog and Veles | Replenishment if you build around Focus, because it spends Adrenaline |
| **Manticore** | Levity, to run Cat School Techniques in Manticore armor | Severance | Placation, which drains the Toxicity that Euphoria feeds on |
| **Viper** | Levity with Cat School Techniques, or Balance on any armor | Greater Chernobog | Placation |

Picks for Feline, Griffin, Ursine and Manticore follow [Console Pulse's ranking](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-runewords-glyphwords-ranked) and its 5.0 builds; the Wolven and Viper rows, and the Avoid column, are this book's reasoning. (Console Pulse also describes a heavy-armor build that uses Levity, which turns it into a Cat build.) The 5.0 Griffin builds skip runewords and use greater Veles stones ([Console Pulse: Griffin Igni build](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-remastered-griffin-igni-pure-sign-build)).

## When to visit

- **Single stones:** from the first merchant who sells them. They survive upgrades, so there's no reason to wait.
- **Tier 1 (5,000 crowns):** worth it mainly for **Heft** (Bear School Techniques on any armor) and **Preservation** (permanent grindstone and armorer's table bonuses).
- **Tier 2 (15,000 total):** Balance, Protection, Entanglement, Severance, Rejuvenation and Elation.
- **Tier 3 (30,000 total):** Levity, Invigoration, Eruption and Replenishment. Do this once your final chest piece is in hand, usually in Toussaint.
