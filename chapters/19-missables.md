# 19. Missables: one-time gear

Most gear in The Witcher 3 waits for you, including every Feline, Griffin, Ursine, Wolven and Grandmaster diagram. A few things don't. This chapter lists the gear you can lose for good, with exactly what loses it. Patch 5.0 changed no items, so pre-5.0 sources apply ([KeenGamer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/witcher-3-remastered-patch-notes-skill-reset-reforge-and-major-changes/)). One thing 5.0 adds: since owning an item or its diagram unlocks its look for Reforge, every missable below is also a look you can never reforge to.

**Make a manual save** before each point of no return marked below.

The chapter ends with the [unmarked quests](#unmarked-quests): 38 small encounters that never appear in your quest log, a few of which hide gear or expire with the story.

## Base game

- [ ] **Winter's Blade** (relic steel). Ask Crach an Craite for help during *Brothers in Arms: Skellige*; he can't send men and gives you the sword instead. Guides say to do this before you sail to the Isle of Mists; none says outright that the quest fails there, but the story goes straight to Kaer Morhen afterwards. The Witcher wiki adds that he only gives it if you helped his children, Cerys in *Possession* and Hjalmar in *The Lord of Undvik*; GameBanshee and Gamer Guides mention no condition. Finish both quests first to be safe ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Brothers_In_Arms:_Skellige); [GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/brothersinarms.php)).
- [ ] **Hjalmar's steel sword.** During *King's Gambit*, win the fistfight in Hjalmar's corner of the Kaer Trolde feast hall before you leave to find Crach ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/kingsgambit.php)).
- [ ] **Rose of Shaerrawed** (silver). While preparing for the Battle of Kaer Morhen, choose the armory in the workshop instead of patching the wall. GameBanshee names the sword; Gamer Guides says the armory gives a random silver sword ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/thebattleofkaermorhen.php)).

**Not missable:** the Feline, Griffin, Ursine and Wolven diagrams. None is tied to a quest that can fail, the open world stays playable after the ending, and the Isle of Mists has no unique loot. Kaer Morhen holds several of them; no source says outright that it stays open after the battle there, but the evidence points that way: Console Pulse calls it accessible from *Ugly Baby* onward, and GameBanshee places Geralt at Kaer Morhen after the ending ([Console Pulse: Wolf gear](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-wolf-school-gear-guide); [GameBanshee: Something Ends, Something Begins](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/somethingendssomethingbegins.php)).

- [ ] **Forgotten Wolven Basic diagrams, to be safe.** They're the reward for the side quest *In the Eternal Fire's Shadow*, and GameBanshee warns that most open base-game side quests fail at the Isle of Mists. No source says whether this one does, so finish it before you sail.

## Hearts of Stone

- [ ] **Viper armor diagrams** (all four). **Countess Mignole** sells them at the auction house at the start of *Open Sesame!*, while Vimme Vivaldi introduces you to the guests. Buy them before the auction ends; you can't return. One old source links them to Yaromir's optional art test; others don't, but the test is free, so do it anyway ([GameBanshee: Open Sesame!](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/opensesame.php); [Console Pulse: HoS missables](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-hearts-of-stone-achievements-missables-guide)).
- [ ] **Auction lots.** Professor's Spectacles (cosmetic), *Starry Night over the Pontar* (with Yaromir's tip, it starts *Avid Collector*) and Count Romilly's Figurine (dismantling it starts *A Dark Legacy*). Bring at least 1,000 crowns.
- [ ] **Viper Venomous steel sword diagram.** In a container in the inner vault during the Borsodi heist. Loot every container before you leave the vault; you can't go back.
- [ ] **Caretaker's Spade** (possibly missable). Dropped by the Caretaker in the garden during *Scenes From a Marriage*. It doesn't glow in Witcher Senses; pick it up before you go into the house, because no source says whether you can come back for it ([Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-remastered-caretaker-spade-iris-aerondight)).
- [ ] **Ornate robe and boots** (relic). In the container next to Iris's body in the master bedroom, same quest.
- [ ] **Viper Venomous silver sword and Iris.** Both depend on the ending of *Whatsoever a Man Soweth…*:
  1. Do the optional step with **Shani and Professor Shakeslock** first, or you won't be offered the challenge.
  2. At the Temple of Lilvani, choose to **help Olgierd** in the timed choice.
  3. In O'Dimm's riddle world, kill the specter at the red-glowing spot and **pull the sword from the stone before you solve the riddle**. GameBanshee gives the timer as about five minutes.
  4. Win, and Olgierd gives you **Iris** back in the real world.

  If you let O'Dimm take Olgierd instead, you get one boon (the Caparison of Lament, the Horn of Plenty, the Bottomless Carafe or 5,000 crowns) and lose both swords ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Whatsoever_a_Man_Soweth...); [GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/whatsoeveramansoweth.php)).

**Not missable:** the Ofieri diagrams (an old bug that could stall the quest was fixed in patch 4.0), the Runewright, and the New Moon relic set in the open world. Only the weaker New Moon copies you wear during the heist are one-time.

## Blood and Wine

- [ ] **Tesham Mutna set** (six pieces). Only during *La Cage au Fou*, in three containers in the ruins on the way to the cage chamber: the chest Regis leans on (armor, trousers, sword), the chest by the door after the stairs beside the hanging cages (mask, boots), and a strongbox on a wall shelf after the second cell (gauntlets) ([GoSuNoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/witcher-3/tesham-mutna-armor/); [Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Tesham_Mutna_armor)).
- [ ] **Tourney prizes** in *The Warble of a Smitten Knight*: a crossbow, a saddle and a leveled sword, plus easy Honor and Valor chances for Aerondight. The quest fails once *Capture the Castle* starts (Console Pulse) or when you find Guillaume's body during *The Night of Long Fangs* (GameBanshee); either way, finish it before the story's route split ([Console Pulse](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-warble-of-a-smitten-knight-guide)).
- [ ] **The route choice in *The Night of Long Fangs*** decides which heavy relic set you can get:

  | Route | You get | It locks when |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | **Find Damien → Syanna** (*Beyond Hill and Dale…*) | The **Toussaint relic** armor, **Vitis** and the found **Gesheft**, all in the Land of a Thousand Fables | You interact with the Land of a Thousand Fables book in the palace playroom wardrobe |
  | **Find Orianna → the Unseen Elder** (*Blood Simple*, then *What Lies Unseen*) | The **Hen Gaidth** set and the **Cantata** silver sword | You tell Regis at the docks that you're going to the Unseen Elder (per Console Pulse) |

  You can follow both leads before you commit. One set per playthrough: a manual save before the choice is the only way to see both ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Night_of_Long_Fangs); [Console Pulse: B&W choices](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/the-witcher-3-choices-blood-and-wine)).
- [ ] **Loot as you go** in the Land of a Thousand Fables and the Unseen Elder's lair. Both close behind you.

**Not missable in practice:** **Aerondight**. Every virtue has several chances, and the quest can be finished after the main story (chapter 18). **Not missable:** every Grandmaster diagram; even Manticore's trial in Murky Delve can't be failed ([Console Pulse: Grandmaster sets](https://consolepulse.com/multiplatform/the-witcher/guides/witcher-3-grandmaster-gear-sets-bonuses-compared)).

## Unmarked quests

Unmarked quests are short encounters with no map marker and, mostly, no journal entry: a bridge toll, a cry for help, a duel. Many start when Geralt walks close enough, and some resolve themselves before you arrive, so save before entering a new area if you want to see them ([game-checklists.com](https://game-checklists.com/witcher3/all-unmarked-quests-checklist.html)). The list below follows the 38 base-game encounters in game-checklists.com's checklist, which its author [announced on r/Witcher3](https://www.reddit.com/r/Witcher3/comments/1xf7jqt/checklist_of_all_unmarked_quests_in_witcher_3/) after replaying the game for them; it says locations and triggers are unchanged in Remastered. Each entry is checked against its Witcher wiki page (pre-5.0), and where the two disagree, both versions are given. *Strangers in the Night* has no wiki page, so it rests on the checklist alone.

**Worth doing for your build:**

- **Mourner** (relic steel sword): sold for 525 crowns, or looted, in *Deadly Crossing II* ([Witcher wiki: Mourner](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Mourner)).
- **Enhanced Ursine boots diagram:** in a chest near the boat in the *Farting Trolls* cave ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Farting_Trolls)).
- **Random diagrams:** two in each of the two bundles at Boris's camp in *Harassing a Troll*, and a couple in the chest under the bridge in *Highwayman's Cache* ([Witcher wiki: Harassing a Troll](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Harassing_a_Troll); [Highwayman's Cache](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Highwayman%27s_Cache)).
- **Merchants:** saving Anselm in *Caravan Attack* adds a trader at Crow's Perch a week later; walking away in *Suspicious Shakedown* makes that merchant refuse to trade with you ([Witcher wiki: Caravan Attack](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Caravan_Attack); [Suspicious Shakedown](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Suspicious_Shakedown)).
- **Axii options** appear in *Deadly Crossing I*, *A Warm Welcome*, *Drunken Rabble*, *Strumpet in Distress*, *Suspicious Shakedown*, *Yustianna Disturbed* and *Face Me If You Dare III*. The wiki gives the pre-5.0 Delusion levels (1 or 2); 5.0 changed the checks, so keep Delusion slotted, at rank 3 to be safe (chapter 2).

**Ones that can expire:**

- *At the Mercy of Strangers I* disappears if you advance too far in the main story (John Verdun is dead by then), and part II needs him alive ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/At_the_Mercy_of_Strangers)).
- *Witch Hunter Raids* appears only after *Pyres of Novigrad* and is gone after *Now or Never* ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Witch_Hunter_Raids)).
- *Face Me If You Dare II* and *III* need Ronvid alive: kill him in part I or II and the chain ends ([game-checklists.com](https://game-checklists.com/witcher3/all-unmarked-quests-checklist.html)).
- *Deadly Crossing III* won't spawn if you did part I more than a few days earlier (the wiki tested a week), and it can fire while you're far away ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Deadly_Crossing)).
- *Never Trust Children* has to be finished, or the child let go, before *Hidden Messages of the Nilfgaardian Kind* becomes available; the encounter vanishes if you leave the area once its marker shows ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Never_Trust_Children)).

### Velen (14)

- [ ] ***At the Mercy of Strangers I*** (Mulbrydale). John Verdun is tied up by the river between Mulbrydale and the Inn at the Crossroads, surrounded by drowners. Untie him to unlock part II. Best done on the way to the Inn during *The Nilfgaardian Connection* ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/At_the_Mercy_of_Strangers)).
- [ ] ***Caravan Attack*** (Marauders' Bridge). Merchants fighting five bandits on the paths by the ponds northwest of the bridge. Save them and Anselm sets up shop in Crow's Perch a week later ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Caravan_Attack)).
- [ ] ***Deadly Crossing I*** (Troll Bridge). Bandits demand a toll: pay 50 crowns, use Axii, or fight. game-checklists.com advises against killing them ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Deadly_Crossing)).
- [ ] ***Deadly Crossing II*** (Lindenvale). Looters on the long bridge north of Lindenvale sell **Mourner**. Visit once while the Nilfgaardians hold the bridge, then come back after they're dead. Approaching with a sword drawn makes them hostile ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Deadly_Crossing)).
- [ ] ***Deadly Crossing III*** (Dragonslayers' Grotto). Openly hostile bandits at a road junction west of the grotto. Do it within a few days of part I (see above).
- [ ] ***Highwayman's Cache*** (Boatmakers' Hut). Three bandits at the bridge east of the hut demand a toll; fight them or show your medallion. Then follow footprints from their campfire with Witcher Senses to a chest under the bridge with diagrams. game-checklists.com treats it as the end of the *Deadly Crossing* chain; the wiki says it isn't officially part of it ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Highwayman%27s_Cache)).
- [ ] ***Harassing a Troll*** (Inn at the Crossroads). Far northwest of the Inn, near the river: help Boris the rock troll against five nekkers, then loot the two bundles by his pot ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Harassing_a_Troll)).
- [ ] ***Face Me If You Dare I*** (Crow's Perch). Ronvid of the Small Marsh challenges you at a crossroads by the bridge into Crow's Perch. Beat him without killing him ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Face_Me_if_You_Dare!)).
- [ ] ***Highway Robbery*** (Boatmakers' Hut). A "trader" calling for help on the road between Crow's Perch and the hut is one of the bandits ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Highway_Robbery)).
- [ ] ***Looters I*** (Heatherton). Five foot looters and three riders on the wooded path east of Heatherton. Looters I to III are independent ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Looters)).
- [ ] ***Looters II*** (Codgers' Quarry). Approach with swords sheathed and they ask for help against alghouls; drawn, they attack. Ignore them long enough and you find one hostile survivor ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Looters)).
- [ ] ***Looters III*** (Toderas). Looters in the abandoned village; kill them or let them kill each other ([game-checklists.com](https://game-checklists.com/witcher3/all-unmarked-quests-checklist.html)).
- [ ] ***Man's Best Friend*** (level 5). Kill the wild dog at a lone hut, then feed the orphans or give them 25 crowns for the book *In Beast's Clothing*. game-checklists.com places the hut northeast of the Inn at the Crossroads and southwest of the Border Post; the wiki says northwest of Mulbrydale ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Man%27s_Best_Friend)).
- [ ] ***The Price of Passage*** (Oxenfurt Harbor). Redanian soldiers under the archway from the harbor to the market ask for a "war tax". Pay and they keep coming back for more; refuse and you're let go. Needs the drunk part of *Contract: The Oxenfurt Drunk* done first ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Price_of_Passage)).

### Novigrad and Oxenfurt (16)

- [ ] ***At the Mercy of Strangers II.*** Only if you freed John Verdun. He and his bandits have raided the refugees who tied him up; take his payment or fight him. game-checklists.com puts the camp near Ursten; the wiki says Grassy Knoll, northwest of the Border Post ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/At_the_Mercy_of_Strangers)).
- [ ] ***A Warm Welcome*** (Wheat Fields). Redanian soldiers turning back refugees on the road southwest of Wheat Fields, toward the river. Help them (100 crowns, Axii, or a fight) and a refugee tells you of a hero's tomb near Downwarren ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/A_Warm_Welcome)).
- [ ] ***Strangers in the Night*** (Carsten). Three bandits on the small peninsula west of Carsten. Checklist only ([game-checklists.com](https://game-checklists.com/witcher3/all-unmarked-quests-checklist.html)).
- [ ] ***The Most Truest of Basilisks*** (Herbalist's Hut, outside Oxenfurt). A beast tamer's caged "basilisk" is a wyvern; call it out and kill it when it escapes. It only appears between 7am and 6pm ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Most_Truest_of_Basilisks)).
- [ ] ***Drunken Rabble*** (Hierarch Square). Two drunk butchers outside the Golden Sturgeon want coin; refuse twice for a fistfight ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Drunken_Rabble)).
- [ ] ***Face Me If You Dare II*** (Tretogor Gate). Ronvid again, southeast of the gate by the Pontar, now with a shield ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Face_Me_if_You_Dare!)).
- [ ] ***Face Me If You Dare III*** (Novigrad Docks area). Ronvid and two hired thugs in Silverton. Only Axii saves all three; any other answer ends in a fight to the death ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Face_Me_if_You_Dare!)).
- [ ] ***Witch Hunter Raids*** (Portside Gate). Between *Pyres of Novigrad* and *Now or Never*: witch hunters break into the mage Remi Villeroy's house. Defend him for runestones ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Witch_Hunter_Raids)).
- [ ] ***Racists of Novigrad I.*** Townsmen harassing an elf woman. game-checklists.com puts it in an alley east of Novigrad Docks; the wiki at the second intersection north of Portside Gate ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Racists_of_Novigrad)).
- [ ] ***Racists of Novigrad II*** (Tretogor Gate). Townsmen attacking elves by the dye pits northeast of the gate. Usually appears only after part I ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Racists_of_Novigrad)).
- [ ] ***Karmic Justice*** (Tretogor Gate). Three elves corner a merchant in the alley north of the dye pits. Talk to them quickly, or they kill him. The wiki says it may be night-only again ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Karmic_Justice)).
- [ ] ***Never Trust Children I*** (Hierarch Square). A crying child by the Crooked House in the Bits leads you into an ambush ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Never_Trust_Children)).
- [ ] ***Strumpet in Distress*** (Novigrad Docks). A man threatening a strumpet south of the signpost. It's a game between them ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Strumpet_in_Distress)).
- [ ] ***Suspicious Shakedown*** (Hierarch Square). After *Pyres of Novigrad*: bandits extort a merchant behind a door north of Rosemary and Thyme. Help him, or he won't trade with you ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Suspicious_Shakedown)).
- [ ] ***The Flame of Hatred I*** (Hierarch Square). An Eternal Fire preacher east of the square insults witchers. Answer him rather than walking away to unlock part II ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Flame_of_Hatred)).
- [ ] ***The Flame of Hatred II.*** A few days later, two guards stop you by a well in the Bits; hand over your weapons or not, it ends in a fight. If you surrendered them, loot them back. game-checklists.com says only embarrassing the preacher triggers this; the wiki says any answer except leaving does ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Flame_of_Hatred)).

### Skellige (7)

- [ ] ***Call of the Wild*** (Arinbjorn). White wolves in the forest east-southeast of Arinbjorn, plus an ulfhedinn at night only. The ulfhedinn doesn't respawn ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Call_of_the_Wild)).
- [ ] ***Farting Trolls*** (Wild Shore). Unmarked cave just west of the Place of Power on the road between Arinbjorn and Kaer Muire. Three rock trolls; the chest near the boat holds the **Enhanced Ursine boots** diagram ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Farting_Trolls)).
- [ ] ***Never Trust Children II*** (Sund). A child on the road between Arinbjorn and Sund leads you into thugs. Independent of part I ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Never_Trust_Children)).
- [ ] ***Siren's Call*** (Sund). A shepherd attacked by sirens on the road west of Sund ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Siren%27s_Call)).
- [ ] ***The Four Faces of Hemdall*** (Trottheim). Pirates blocking a pilgrim at a shrine northwest of Trottheim on Faroe. If you killed Hammond in *Following the Thread*, they attack on sight ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Four_Faces_of_Hemdall)).
- [ ] ***Woe is Me*** (Palisade, Ard Skellig). A "wounded" peasant and two hidden robbers. It only triggers if you approach from the west ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Woe_is_Me)).
- [ ] ***Yustianna Disturbed*** (Yustianna's Grotto). Bandits in the grotto's central chamber. Take the right-hand path to get the conversation; pay 150 crowns, use Axii, or fight ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Yustianna_Disturbed)).

### Kaer Morhen (1)

- [ ] ***Trail of Echoes.*** The cave at the end of the road northwest of the keep, the same one *Scavenger Hunt: Wolf School Gear* sends you to. Kill the nekkers and devourer, then examine the equipment and the Trial of the Grasses registry ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Trail_of_Echoes)).

## Points of no return in the main story

- **The Isle of Mists.** Near the arch, the game asks you to make a manual save, and GameBanshee warns that most open base-game side quests fail when you continue. Guides treat it as the deadline for Winter's Blade and the Forgotten Wolven quest too. Finish all the *Brothers in Arms* quests before you sail ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/theisleofmists.php); [Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/the-wild-hunt/the-isle-of-mists)).
- **The final one:** speaking to Avallac'h next to the boat during *On Thin Ice* ([Witcher wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/On_Thin_Ice)).
- **After the ending** you return to the open world and can keep playing contracts, treasure hunts and side quests ([Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/something-ends-something-begins)), and start both expansions from there ([GameBanshee](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher3/walkthrough/somethingendssomethingbegins.php)).
